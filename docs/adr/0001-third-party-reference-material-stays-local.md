# Third-party reference material stays local; STE rules are written in our own words

The source post, its image, and the ASD-STE100 specification belong to other people, and the specification is free to download but not licensed for redistribution. We keep originals only in the git-ignored `reference/local/`, commit a link plus our own summary and short attributed quotes, and write every STE rule, example, and word list in our own words with a "not official, not endorsed by ASD" note. We chose this before the first commit because git history becomes public with the repo and cannot be taken back.

## Consequences

- No skill can ship the official ~900-word STE dictionary, so "strict" STE here means our rules plus our own replacement list, not certified ASD-STE100.
