Prior to the replacement of the battery module in the portable power unit, it is of the utmost importance that the unit is completely disconnected from the mains supply and that the power switch has been placed in the off position, because contact with the internal terminals while the unit is energized can result in a severe electric shock to the technician. The module cover is removed by loosening the four retaining screws, after which the old module is carefully lifted out of the housing. Metal tools must not be allowed to touch the terminals, since a short circuit that is caused by such contact may lead to overheating of the cells and damage to the charging circuit. The new module is inserted until a click is heard, the cover is refitted, and the screws are tightened.
