The order processing system is composed of three loosely coupled services that communicate by means of an asynchronous message queue. The intake service, which is responsible for the validation of incoming customer orders, publishes a validated order event to the queue, and this event is subsequently consumed by both the inventory service and the payment service, each of which operates independently of the other. In the event that the payment service is temporarily unavailable, the pending events are retained in the queue until such time as the service has recovered, so that no customer order is lost. A shared order status database, which is updated by all three services, is utilized by the customer notification component in order to determine which status messages are to be sent to the customer.
